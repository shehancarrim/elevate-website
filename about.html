About Raween Osama

Hello, I’m Raween Osama, a marketer, career branding specialist, educator, storyteller, trainer, and event host.

My professional journey has taken me across the MENA region, Turkey, and India. While my career has evolved in many directions, some passions have remained constant throughout my life: a love for reading, cross-cultural human connections, storytelling, public speaking, and continuous personal growth. Today, these passions are at the heart of everything I do.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Integrated Marketing Communications and a Global Master of Business Administration, complemented by professional certifications in marketing, education, project management, graphic design, and other fields. Over the years, I have built experience across many industries, including publishing, education, entrepreneurship, FMCG, and supply chain, giving me a well-rounded perspective and the ability to adapt to different audiences and customer needs.

After six years of working full-time with multinational corporations, universities, and non-profit organizations, I decided to pursue freelancing and entrepreneurship full-time. This step was driven by purpose: the desire to expand my impact within communities, have better control over my schedule, and dedicate more time to my family, and the work I genuinely love.
