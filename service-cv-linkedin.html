Services

CV & LinkedIn Development

Your CV and LinkedIn profile are often the first impression you make on potential employers. Whether you're a student, an early-career professional, a highly experienced executive, or returning to work after a career break, I can help you!

I have worked alongside HR professionals to develop CVs and optimize LinkedIn profiles for clients worldwide. My goal is to create a CV and LinkedIn profile that highlight your strengths, showcase your value, and help you stand out to recruiters and hiring managers.

All packages are designed to optimize your CV and LinkedIn profile for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), increasing your chances of passing initial screenings and attracting recruiter attention.
